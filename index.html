Frequently Asked Questions

What minimum monthly ad budget do you work with? +
We typically work with brands allocating ₹1 Lakh+ monthly ad spend on Meta or Google Ads. This ensures sufficient data volume for algorithmic optimization and reliable A/B testing.
Do you handle vertical 9:16 reel shoots in-house? +
Yes! We have an in-house production crew in Ahmedabad equipped for studio shoots, food styling, reel scripting, and vernacular Gujarati/Hindi voiceovers.
Are long-term lock-in contracts required? +
We operate on flexible 90-day growth cycles. This gives campaigns enough runway to stabilize while keeping us accountable to real ROAS and lead targets.
How fast can campaigns go live after onboarding? +
Once access is granted, initial creative shoots and campaign setups take 5 to 7 business days. Ads typically start driving live traffic within week one.
How do you track store footfalls and offline sales? +
We combine Meta Store Location targeting with custom WhatsApp voucher redemptions and QR tracking at billing counters to attribute in-store visits directly to ad campaigns.
