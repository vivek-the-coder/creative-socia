How We Build Predictable Growth Campaigns

From initial brand audit in Vadodara to full-scale Meta & Google ad distribution across Gujarat, our systematic pipeline delivers consistent ROAS.

[PHASE 01 · DAYS 1–4]

Audience & Hook Strategy

Location: Vadodara / Ahmedabad Studio

We analyze your target consumer segments in Gujarat, existing CRM purchase history, and competitor ad fatigue. Our directors map out 6 to 12 distinct video hook angles (e.g., texture closeups, regional humor, price-to-value comparisons) specifically designed for Meta 9:16 reels.

✓ Hook Matrix Workshop
✓ Audience Persona Mapping
✓ Scripting (Gujarati/Hindi)
[PHASE 02 · DAYS 5–10]

In-Studio 9:16 Reel Shoots

Location: Alkapuri Plaza / Titanium Heights

Our production team executes cinema-grade vertical shoots using high-speed lighting, macro lenses, and authentic local creators. We record multiple intro hooks, call-to-actions, and background tracks so ads can be dynamically mixed for algorithmic optimization.

✓ 4K Vertical Cinema Rig
✓ High-Speed Food/Product Shoots
✓ Native Voiceovers & Audio
[PHASE 03 · DAYS 11–14]

Meta & Google Media Buying

Location: Active Ads Deployment

We deploy targeted Meta Reel Ads and Google Performance Max campaigns. Ad budgets are structured with tight geo-radii around your physical stores or distributed across high-density Gujarat postal codes to maximize impression frequency without ad burn.

✓ Geo-Radius Outlet Ads
✓ Lookalike Audience Setup
✓ Automated Placement Mix
[PHASE 04 · ONGOING]

Attribution & Scale Loop

Location: Weekly ROAS Review

We track footfalls, WhatsApp inquiries, and direct store sales. Every 14 days, we review ad frequency metrics; when fatigue appears, our studio produces fresh creative iterations within 48 hours to maintain high ROAS.

✓ WhatsApp POS Attribution
✓ Weekly ROAS Dashboard
✓ Rapid Creative Refresh
